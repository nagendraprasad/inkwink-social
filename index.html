InkWink Social

InkWink Social is a private tool used only by the owners of the InkWinkStudio, Mr & Mrs Behara and InkWinkLabs channels. It uploads our own videos to our own YouTube channels and reads their statistics for our own reports. It is not offered to anyone else, and no one else can sign in to it.

YouTube API Services

InkWink Social uses YouTube API Services (YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API) to upload videos to, and read statistics from, the owners' own YouTube channels: @inkwinkstudio, @mrmrsbehara and @inkwinklabs. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service.
