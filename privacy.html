Privacy policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

InkWink Social is a private tool used only by the owners of the InkWinkStudio, Mr & Mrs Behara and InkWinkLabs channels to manage their own channels. It uses YouTube API Services.

What it accesses

When a channel owner signs in with Google, InkWink Social can upload videos and set titles, descriptions, tags and thumbnails on that owner's channel, and read that channel's statistics (views, watch time, likes, comments, subscribers).

How the data is used and stored

The data is used only to publish the owners' videos and to show the owners how their videos perform. Sign-in tokens and statistics are stored only on the owners' own computer. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising. No data about viewers is collected beyond the aggregate statistics YouTube provides to channel owners.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at Google account permissions. Stored data for a channel is deleted when its owner removes access or asks for it to be deleted.

Google and YouTube

By using InkWink Social you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.
